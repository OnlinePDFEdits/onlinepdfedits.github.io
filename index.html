Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our browser-based PDF suite.

Is OnlinePDFEdits really free? ▼
Yes, completely. There are no hidden paywalls, no trial expirations, and no credit card requirements. All tools including text editing, eSignatures, compression, and conversion are free to use.
Are my files private and secure? ▼
We use client-assisted WebAssembly and ephemeral server processing. Your documents are never kept in long-term cloud storage, never indexed, and never shared with third parties.
Can I edit existing text or just add new text? ▼
You can edit existing text! Unlike basic tools that only let you type on top of a page, OnlinePDFEdits identifies existing text runs and lets you fix typos, update dates, or edit paragraphs while preserving font families and alignments.
Does it work on Mac, Windows, Linux, and Mobile? ▼
Yes! OnlinePDFEdits runs on any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without installing software or extensions.
How do I submit feedback or report a bug? ▼
We maintain an open community feedback repository on GitHub! You can submit bug reports, vote on feature requests, and follow development at github.com/OnlinePDFEdits/feedback.
