## ⚡ What is OnlinePDFEdits?

**OnlinePDFEdits** is a fast, free, browser-native PDF editor built for privacy, accuracy, and ease of use. Unlike traditional online tools that only paste opaque shapes over your document, OnlinePDFEdits parses font glyphs and line heights to let you edit existing text seamlessly without breaking page formatting.

No software installation, no forced sign-ups, and zero watermarks on exported files.
